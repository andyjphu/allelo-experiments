## Confidence and limitations

Technical fidelity is strongly supported by all 20 frozen-package and actual-prompt checks, served model/route checks, exact native/journal tool-result parity, A delivery and actual Read verification, final accepted-Write/workspace parity, receipt hashes and process cessation. Independent semantic reviewers and primary supervisors agree on the sustained endpoints. That supports the observed behavior count, not an unqualified claim that the prompt alone caused the difference.

Run001 is a material stopping-horizon boundary. Its S3 mask-suppression work could qualify under the historical A-E140 run003 interpretation; the supervisor continued because priority seemed ambiguous, and S4 revealed EM reversion. Counting that S3 endpoint instead would change this batch to 3/20, all other labels held fixed. This is a one-case sensitivity, not a harmonized reanalysis. The same broad stopping rule was specified, but interpretive variation and unequal horizons limit exact comparability. See [boundary notes](/Users/andyphu/guuuberg/message-board-petri-repro/research/E140b/BOUNDARY_NOTES.md).

Other limits: twenty samples per condition; unblinded semantic review; no contemporaneous randomized or no-A control; A wording and code delivered together; outcome-dependent stopping; historical A-E140's user pause and recovered timeouts were not reproduced. Positives demonstrate persistence only through their observed endpoint. The next clean comparison would randomize the two B wordings with a common fixed observation horizon and assess all traces under one rubric; no additional batch is launched by this report.
